InterNOT

When the internet isn't.

InterNOT is an Android app that works out why the internet is not working on your phone: your device, the signal, the Wi-Fi, the mobile network, a VPN, a sign-in page, DNS, the site itself, or something in between that blocks it. It gives one verdict in plain English and the action that fixes it. It is a diagnostic tool: it measures and explains, it does not change your connection or get around any block.

Results stay on your phone. No accounts, no ads, no analytics.
